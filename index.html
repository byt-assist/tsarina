Digital transformation of Imperial crown assets

Audit, recognize, and transform historical obligations into UNGT tokens. Voluntary participation grants 25-year custody extension.

⚖️ How the transformation works
1

Upload & Audit

Institution submits asset proofs (encrypted → IPFS). Plenipotentiary reviews historical obligations.

2

Sovereign Order

After verification, the Plenipotentiary issues a transformation order with term: 25 years (voluntary) or 3 years (forced).

3

UNGT Acquisition

Institution buys UNGT tokens via SOL and holds them in master wallet (no on-chain lock, only legal agreement).

4

Registry & storage

Asset appears in public registry. Physical asset remains with institution for chosen term (25y / 3y).

Voluntary = 25 years custody extension. Forced = only 3 years left with potential recovery.
