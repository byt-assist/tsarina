About the Tsarina UNGT Project

Restoring historical justice through digital transformation of Imperial Crown assets

Our Mission

The UNGT (Unredeemed Note of the Great Tsarina) project is a digital transformation initiative aimed at auditing, recognizing, and tokenizing historical obligations and assets of the Imperial Crown. We provide a transparent, blockchain-based mechanism for financial institutions to voluntarily digitize their holdings while maintaining physical custody.

Legal Framework

Based on Consulting Agreement #07022024ZRU598 and Power of Attorney from March 5, 2024, the project operates under the authority of the Suzerain (Heir of the Imperial Crown) and the Plenipotentiary (authorized representative).
