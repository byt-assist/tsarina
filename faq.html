Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about UNGT token and asset digitization

What is UNGT token?
UNGT (Unredeemed Note of the Great Tsarina) is a Solana-based token representing digital transformation of historical Imperial Crown assets and obligations.
Who can participate?
Banks, trusts, and financial institutions holding assets related to the Imperial Crown. Voluntary participation is encouraged with significant benefits.
What are the benefits of voluntary transformation?
3% fee on asset value, 22 years usage rights (credit/collateral leverage), 15% buyback discount, and 25 years custody extension.
What happens during forced transformation?
Forced transformation results in 3 years custody only, no fee, no usage rights, and no buyback discount.
How are documents stored?
Documents are client-side encrypted and stored on IPFS/Arweave via Pinata. Only the Plenipotentiary can view original documents.
How to buy UNGT tokens?
You can buy UNGT on Jupiter aggregator (swap SOL for UNGT) or directly through our website's "Buy on Jupiter" button.
Is the smart contract audited?
The smart contract is publicly verifiable on Solana blockchain. Mint Authority and Freeze Authority are properly configured for transparency.
Who is the Suzerain?
The Suzerain is Svetlana Nikolaevna Aristova, Heir of the Imperial Crown, acting as legal successor and ultimate authority over the digitization process.
